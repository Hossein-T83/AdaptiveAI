As a programmer, I direct my own learning through research and experimentation, so that I deepen and broaden the knowledge and skills I can carry forward in my role and career.

## 🔍 Expected Evidence

Learning log account of what was learned (in the team and/or personal project), the resources used (papers, tutorials, experiments, AI tools), explanations in own words, and any work strong enough to show in a portfolio.

## 🧠 ILO 2: Knowledge & Self-Directed Learning

<a name="poor"></a>
🔴 **[ILO 2 Poor]**

> Lists the concrete concepts, methods or skills learned during the block.

<a name="insufficient"></a>
🟠 **[ILO 2 Insufficient]**

> Explains how that learning happened. For example by reading a paper, following a tutorial, running an experiment, or using AI tools to explore a topic.

<a name="sufficient"></a>
🟡 **[ILO 2 Sufficient]**

> Shows genuine understanding of the concepts: explains them in own words and how they work, not just that they were used.

<a name="good"></a>
🟢 **[ILO 2 Good]**

> Goes beyond the minimum needed to solve the problem or deliver the result: engages deeply and broadly with  learning resources, rather than relying only on trial and error or just following a single resource.

<a name="excellent"></a>
🔵 **[ILO 2 Excellent]**

> Applies and shares the gained knowledge with peers, guilds or learning communities, demonstrating it transfers beyond the context in which it was learned.
