As a programmer, I plan, track and adapt my workflow, so that I deliver meaningful work and can account for my progress.

## 🔍 Expected Evidence

Task list with estimated and tracked time and a clear commit history (e.g. Jira and version control), explanations of why significant tasks took the time they did, and evidence of keeping work moving and adapting to change.

## 📈 ILO 3: Workflow & Productivity

<a name="poor"></a>
🔴 **[ILO 3 Poor]**

> Makes output visible and accountable: maintains a task list with estimated and tracked time and  keeps a clear commit history.

<a name="insufficient"></a>
🟠 **[ILO 3 Insufficient]**

> Explains why significant tasks took the time they did rather than only reporting that they were done.Taking complexity, unforeseen issues, research and iteration, or dependencies into consideration.

<a name="sufficient"></a>
🟡 **[ILO 3 Sufficient]**

> Keeps work moving: delivers consistently through the block, resolves or escalates blockers promptly rather than letting them sit, and adjusts plans and priorities in response to changing requirements or new information.

<a name="good"></a>
🟢 **[ILO 3 Good]**

> Anticipates change rather than only reacting to it: flags risks early, plans for unexpected delays, and re-plans proactively to stay ahead of problems.

<a name="excellent"></a>
🔵 **[ILO 3 Excellent]**

> Defines and refines an efficient workflow that delivers an exceptional volume of meaningful work, driving the project forward.
